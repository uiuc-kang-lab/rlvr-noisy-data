6. The six runs

NUM_GPUS already defaults to 4, so no --num_gpus needed.

D=deepscaler_train_pr-noise-0.25.parquet
M=Qwen/Qwen2.5-Math-7B
R="--model=$M --base_dir=$BASE_DIR --train_data=$D"

bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler.sh        $R --run_name=grpo-0.25
bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler_pgfc.sh   $R --run_name=pgfc-0.25 --pgfc_noise_rate=0.25
bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler_drgrpo.sh $R --run_name=drgrpo-0.25
bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler_sapo.sh   $R --run_name=sapo-0.25
bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler_dapo.sh   $R --run_name=dapo-0.25
bash rl_noise/math/run_deepscaler_tis.sh    $R --run_name=tis-0.25

Run these one at a time, not concurrently — colocate_all=true means each claims all 4 GPUs. So one 4×H100 box gives you one algorithm at a time; six boxes to parallelize. resume_mode=latest with
per-run_name ckpt paths means a killed run resumes where it stopped.

Two corrections to what I said earlier

Use run_deepscaler_pgfc.sh, never run_deepscaler.sh --pgfc_noise_rate=. Both accept that flag, but on the base script it still routes to environment.skyrl_gym.math_hard.noise_rate — the inert
reward-shift path. It won't error, it'll just silently reproduce GRPO. The dedicated script routes to pgfc_fn_rate and pins the env rate to 0.

Don't align the epoch counts. I told you to earlier; that was wrong. GRPO/Dr.GRPO/SAPO/PGFC use 5 epochs, TIS 50, DAPO 100 — and at 12,769 rows / batch 64 = 199 steps/epoch, 5 epochs is already
~997 steps against a paper x-axis of ~600. DAPO and TIS need far more epochs to reach the same step count because dynamic_sampling.type=filter discards whole batches. The differing counts are
deliberate. Compare on training steps, as the paper's figures do, and leave epochs alone.
